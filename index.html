NothingLost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version

NothingLost keeps a copy of your notifications on your phone so you can read them later. Your notifications and messages never leave your phone. To improve the app, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings.

What the app reads

  • With your permission (Android's "notification access"), NothingLost reads notifications as they arrive: the app they came from, the title, the text and the time.
  • It stores them in a private database inside the app on your phone.
  • To keep running on phones that close background apps, it shows a small silent notification. It also reads Android's own record of when and why the app was closed, on your phone only, so it can tell you if protection was off.

What the app sends

NothingLost uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to learn how the app is used and to fix crashes. They receive:

  • Which screens are opened and which buttons are used (for example "opened the Deleted tab", "tapped Unlock"), and whether setup steps were completed.
  • That a deleted or changed message was caught, without its content.
  • Crash reports: technical details of the error.
  • Device information: phone maker and model, Android version, app version, language, and a random app-instance ID created by Firebase. Google derives an approximate country from the connection.

They never receive notification or message text, sender names, contacts, what you search for, or which apps you keep notifications from. The app does not collect the advertising ID.

Turn this off at any time in Settings → "Share anonymous usage stats"; nothing more is sent after that. Google processes this data under its privacy policy and Firebase privacy terms. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload, share, sell or back up your notifications or messages.
  • Your saved notifications are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no selling of data.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. NothingLost only learns whether the unlock was bought.

Your control

  • Choose which apps are kept, delete everything from Settings, or remove notification access at any time in Android Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all saved data.
  • Turning off "Share anonymous usage stats" also resets the random app-instance ID, so earlier statistics can no longer be linked to this phone. They are deleted automatically after 14 months.
  • Notifications older than one year are deleted automatically.

Children

NothingLost is not directed at children under 13.
